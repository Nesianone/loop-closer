What changed from the current version

  • Bigger, heavier headings. Screen titles ("Today", "Loops") now use a heavy weight with tighter letter-spacing, so the app has a real visual anchor instead of a plain heading.
  • Colour-coded stat tiles. Streak, this-week check-ins, and open tasks each get their own tile and colour, at a glance — mirrors how the reference app surfaces numbers up top.
  • Section dividers. Small caps label + rule, used to group "Active Loop" from "Quick tasks", and similar groupings on other screens — gives the eye a resting point.
  • Rounder, deeper cards. 20px corners instead of 10px, a touch more padding, a subtle border instead of a flat fill.
  • Pill-shaped action buttons inside cards rather than full-width rectangular buttons — reads lighter, still meets the 44px tap target.
  • Icons in the bottom nav, not just text labels — quicker to scan, feels like a "real" app.
  • Kept: your indigo as the core brand colour (just added a cyan and amber for tile variety), and every existing screen/flow/rule — this is a visual pass only, nothing about how the app behaves changes.
